# Episode 1 - The Click That Answered

Jack and Karega are trying to recover a missing file in their ruined server room.
Jack insists his single click could not have caused the damage, while Karega wants
him to explain exactly what he moved. As they retrace the action, the computer
plays a click before Jack touches anything. They test it again, arguing as their
confidence turns into unease, and realize the machine is anticipating them rather
than replaying the past. Jack takes both hands away from the controls. The cursor
still drags the missing file across the screen. End with another click from the
computer and Karega realizing that someone, or something, is making the next move.
