# Episode 6 - The Confirmation

Karega refuses to continue until both agree on one clear action. Jack finally listens instead of clicking ahead. They carefully describe their plan to each other, then wait in silence rather than act when the computer expects. The console makes its prediction too early and exposes the genuine transfer route. End with Jack asking Karega for confirmation, and Karega deliberately choosing not to answer yet.
