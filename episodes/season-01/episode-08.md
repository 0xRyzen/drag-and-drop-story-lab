# Episode 8 - After the Click

With the room quiet, Jack and Karega verify their result rather than celebrating immediately. Their awkward but honest conversation shows that trust, not a faster click, made the difference. They set the single data drive safely on the existing console in a clearly visible action and step back. The missing file appears recovered, but a faint click comes from the idle machine after both stop speaking. End on their shared look: the immediate problem is solved, yet the source of the first prediction remains unknown.
