# Episode 7 - The Unmade Move

Jack holds his position and waits for Karega instead of taking the predicted action. The machine repeatedly signals for a click, but neither obeys. They realize they can make an unpredicted choice together without giving the system an argument to follow. Karega confirms only when the false signal stops. They complete one deliberate file move while preserving the same physical drive and room. End with the console losing its predictive rhythm and the drive indicator settling.
