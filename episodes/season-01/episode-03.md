# Episode 3 - The Wrong Answer

Karega wants to inspect the returned file without opening it, while Jack wants a quick answer. They take turns describing contradictory next moves to confuse the predictive console. Their improvised argument becomes personal, revealing why Karega mistrusts a single unconfirmed action. When Jack finally admits he does not know what will happen, the system briefly stops anticipating them. End with a real opportunity to inspect the file while the room falls unnaturally quiet.
