# Episode 2 - Do Not Touch

Jack and Karega agree to leave the console untouched, but it continues reacting to their argument. Karega notices the lights change when they make a decision, not when they move a hand. Jack tries to fool the machine by saying one thing and doing nothing. For a moment it works, until Karega answers honestly and the missing file returns with a second signal hidden inside it. End with the two realizing that their disagreement is helping the computer predict them.
