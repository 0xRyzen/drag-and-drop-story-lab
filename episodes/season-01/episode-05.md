# Episode 5 - A Place to Hide

Jack and Karega try to move the genuine file onto their one existing black data drive. Jack keeps the drive in one hand while Karega directs the sequence; no duplicate drive or new equipment appears. Their cooperation nearly succeeds, but Jack reacts to an alarming pulse and breaks their agreed timing. The drive indicator lights before the transfer is complete. End with uncertainty about whether they have captured the file or invited it into the drive.
