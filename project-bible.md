# DRAG & DROP - Story Lab Project Bible

This is a NEW TEST continuity. It does not revise the existing production season.
Use existing approved visual assets; the episode descriptions in this repository
are the only plot source. Do not fetch old screenplays or production checkpoints.

## World and style
A damaged server room, one surviving computer console, and two people who disagree
about whether an action should ever happen without confirmation. Serious mystery
with restrained dry humor. Original 2D hand-drawn mystery-adventure cartoon: bold
clean outlines, flat cel shading, expressive compact characters, atmospheric
painterly backgrounds, cold blue/red emergency lighting, subtle smoke and dust.
No 3D CGI, glossy plastic shading, photorealism, or extra physical people.

## Characters and voices
- Jack: @jack-sheet, voice @Grady. Decisive, impatient, dry understatement. Preserve
  the approved sheet rather than generating a new design. Approximately 92% of
  Karega's height at equal depth; do not force a ratio across different perspectives.
- Karega: @karega-sheet, voice @Arthur. Observant, skeptical, deliberate. Preserve
  the approved sheet and compact proportions.
Exactly one Jack and one Karega exist. Off-screen remains the same physical person.

## Locations and props
- @ruined-server-room: approved room, damaged racks and existing surviving console.
- @black-data-drive: one small matte-black rectangular drive with one red indicator.
  Only include it when the episode needs it. Do not invent duplicate drives.
- The existing console includes keyboard, screen and controls; it is not a new asset.
No new locations, recurring characters, or prop designs are automatically created
by this test workflow. A missing required Element stops preflight.

## Physical continuity
Establish a clear room layout in each episode. Jack works on the aisle side of the
console, his body entirely outside the cabinet; Karega stands separately nearby.
Maintain floor contact, two connected arms and two hands per person. Show any
movement and prop handoff. Native 2D acting and readable silhouettes take priority
over intricate close-up hand movements.

## Audio
Jack remains Grady; Karega remains Arthur. English dialogue, native lip sync,
consistent original low instrumental tension motif, server hum, ventilation,
motivated clicks/relay sounds. Dialogue > SFX > quiet music. No narrator or singing.

## Production
MiniMax H3; 2K 1440x2560; 9:16; 15-second clips; batch 1. Choose 4-8 clips per episode
(60-120 seconds) according to the story. Do not reuse an old episode end frame.
